# Промпты эталонных экранов

## Gameplay

Premium polished 2D casual mobile game UI for Fruito Pao, landscape 720×405.
Compact capsule HUD, calm blurred tropical lagoon, translucent teal board,
dense but readable 12×6 grid of large fruit icons on aqua/mint/coral/lemon
glossy rounded tiles. Reserve the bottom 14% as a completely empty native
banner safe-zone. No logo, watermark or UI inside the banner area.

## Menu

Premium polished 2D casual mobile game menu for Fruito Pao, landscape 720×405.
Soft tropical lagoon, fruit logo on the left, three large rounded buttons on
the right: CLASSIC aqua, TIME coral, GROUP lemon. Sound controls top-left,
privacy link bottom-left. Bright, minimal, readable on a small phone, no ad
banner on the menu, no watermark.
